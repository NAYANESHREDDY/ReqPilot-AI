Warehouse Logistics & Fleet Dispatch System

2. User Authentication and Terminal Access
- Logistics operators and warehouse dispatchers should be able to log in securely using corporate multi-factor authentication.
- The system should enforce role-based access control restricting warehouse forklift drivers to assigned loading bays.
- Supervisory credentials and manager override approval should be required before overriding dangerous cargo safety limits.
- System sessions should automatically expire after 15 minutes of terminal inactivity on ruggedized handheld scanners.

3. Dock Scheduling and Route Dispatch
- Freight carriers should be able to book loading dock appointments and reserve freight arrival time slots.
- Warehouse supervisors should be able to schedule outbound freight delivery routes and allocate trucks to delivery windows.
- The system should prevent a loading bay marked unavailable for maintenance from accepting dock reservations.
- Dock appointments should automatically expire if the transport vehicle fails to check in within 30 minutes of scheduled arrival.

4. Automated Driver and Warehouse Notifications
- The system should send automated SMS alerts to freight drivers when their designated loading dock is ready.
- Warehouse managers should receive instant push notifications when a temperature-controlled cold-storage container experiences a temperature anomaly.
- Automated email notifications should be dispatched to shippers when customs clearance paperwork is rejected.
- The system should dispatch alert notifications to terminal staff when dangerous goods dwell time exceeds permissible safety limits.

5. Pallet Inventory and Data Management
- The system should maintain persistent inventory records of pallet barcode, SKU, aisle location, bin number, weight, and expiry date.
- Immutable database audit logs should preserve every pallet movement and bin transfer for at least 7 years for regulatory compliance.
- The system should record forklift equipment telematics, battery charging cycles, and inspection history in the central database.
- Database backup and disaster recovery replication should ensure zero data loss for freight manifests across secondary storage clusters.

6. Operational Performance Analytics and Reporting
- Operations executives should be able to generate quarterly freight throughput reports and warehouse capacity utilization analytics.
- The system should calculate average dock turnaround times and carrier on-time delivery statistical distributions.
- Logistics managers should be able to export carbon emission summaries and fleet fuel consumption reports.
- The system should generate carrier demurrage charge analytics and freight invoice penalty reconciliation summaries.
