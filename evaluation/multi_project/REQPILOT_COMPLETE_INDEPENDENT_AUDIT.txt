Detailed breakdown of why each domain scored below 90% and prioritized fixes that generalize:

1. HOSPITAL MANAGEMENT (Reproduced: 50.81% | Gap to 90%: 39.19% | 61 Errors)
   - Confirmed Root Cause 1: 27 Requirements expected 'UNASSIGNED_OR_NULL'.
     * The hospital specification includes extensive clinical functionality (electronic prescriptions,
       lab test orders, radiology results, patient vitals).
     * The ground truth assigned these 27 requirements to 'UNASSIGNED_OR_NULL'.
     * The LLM, instructed to map every requirement to the best architectural module, mapped them to
       'DATA' (16 times) or 'BOOKING' (3 times). This accounts for 27 of 61 errors (44.3% of all errors).
   - Confirmed Root Cause 2: Architecture emitted 'APPOINTMENT' instead of canonical 'BOOKING'.
     * ArchitectureAgent synthesized 'MOD-APPOINTMENT' ('Appointment Scheduling Service').
     * The LLM mapped 9 appointment requirements to 'MOD-APPOINTMENT'.
     * The official scorer's CANONICAL_MODULE_MAP does not include 'APPOINTMENT' under 'BOOKING'.
     * Result: 9 requirements were marked as mismatches against gold 'BOOKING'.
   - Recommended Generalizable Fix:
     * Update prompt instructions in task_agent.py to allow an explicit 'UNASSIGNED' assignment when
       a requirement represents domain business logic that has no corresponding service module in candidate list.
     * In architecture_agent.py, map appointment scheduling capabilities into canonical 'Booking & Scheduling'.

2. EV CHARGING (Reproduced: 74.14% | Gap to 90%: 15.86% | 15 Errors)
   - Confirmed Root Cause: Boundary Ambiguities in Domain Modules:
     * 'CHARGING-STATION' vs 'CHARGING-SESSION' vs 'BOOKING' vs 'MAINTENANCE'.
     * FR-009: Invoicing charging history -> Expected CHARGING-SESSION, Predicted PAYMENT.
     * FR-013: Operator viewing active reservations -> Expected BOOKING, Predicted CHARGING-SESSION.
     * FR-015: Operator disabling charger -> Expected CHARGING-STATION, Predicted MAINTENANCE.
     * FR-016: Station revenue reporting -> Expected CHARGING-STATION, Predicted REPORTING.
     * FR-031: Charger unavailable on reservation -> Expected BOOKING, Predicted CHARGING-STATION.
   - Recommended Generalizable Fix:
     * Provide clear capability descriptions in the prompt: clarify that entity lifecycle operations
       (disabling, registering, configuring stations) belong to the primary entity service (Charging Station),
       while reservation state belong to Booking, and real-time charging meters belong to Charging Session.

3. E-COMMERCE PLATFORM (Reproduced: 83.33% | Gap to 90%: 6.67% | 6 Errors)
   - Confirmed Root Cause: Cross-cutting Order/Inventory/Payment Boundary:
     * FR-020 (Reserve stock) -> Expected DATA, Predicted BOOKING.
